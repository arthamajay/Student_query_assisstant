COLLEGE: CVR College of Engineering
SECTION: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is CVR College of Engineering?
A: CVR College of Engineering is a leading private engineering college in Telangana, India. It is NAAC 'A' Grade accredited, NBA accredited for 7 programs, UGC Autonomous, and AICTE approved. It is located in Vastunagar, Mangalpalli Village, Ibrahimpatnam, Ranga Reddy District, Telangana - 501510. EAMCET code is CVRH.

Q: What is the EAMCET code for CVR College?
A: The EAMCET code for CVR College of Engineering is CVRH.

Q: What B.Tech programs does CVR offer?
A: CVR offers 12 B.Tech programs: Civil Engineering (60 seats), CSBS (60 seats), CSE (660 seats), CSE AI&ML (240 seats), CSE Data Science (240 seats), CSE Cyber Security (180 seats), ECE (120 seats), Electronics VLSI (60 seats), EEE (60 seats), EIE (60 seats), IT (120 seats), and Mechanical Engineering (60 seats).

Q: What M.Tech programs does CVR offer?
A: CVR offers 6 M.Tech programs: Artificial Intelligence (18 seats), Data Sciences (18 seats), Electrical Power Engineering (18 seats), Embedded Systems (18 seats), Structural Engineering (18 seats), and VLSI Design (18 seats).

Q: Does CVR offer Ph.D programs?
A: Yes. CVR has Ph.D research centres in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE).

Q: What is the highest placement package at CVR?
A: The highest package offered was Rs. 72 LPA by Ethereum Foundation in 2024-25. Other notable packages include Rs. 55 LPA by Google (2025-26), Rs. 57.5 LPA by Deutsche Bank (2022-23), and Rs. 47 LPA by Microsoft (2021-22).

Q: How many students get placed from CVR each year?
A: Placements vary by year. In 2021-22, over 2106 students were placed (nearly 100%). In 2022-23, 1503+ were placed. In 2025-26, 1251+ students were placed with 90+ companies visiting campus.

Q: Which companies recruit from CVR?
A: Major recruiters include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Accenture, Oracle, ServiceNow, Commvault, JP Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, PwC, Cadence, PayPal, Walmart, IBM, SAP, and many more.

Q: Is CVR accredited by NBA?
A: Yes, CVR is NBA accredited for 7 UG programs (Tier 1).

Q: What is CVR's NAAC grade?
A: CVR College of Engineering holds NAAC 'A' Grade.

Q: Does CVR have a Minor Degree program?
A: Yes. B.Tech students from 3rd year onwards can pursue a Minor Degree in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, or IoT by completing 18 additional credits beyond the 160 major credits.

Q: What awards has CVR received?
