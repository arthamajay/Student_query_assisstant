--- MINOR DEGREE PROGRAMS ---

Available Minor Specializations: AI and Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Data Science, Internet of Things (IoT)

Eligibility: B.Tech students from 3rd year onwards who have completed 160 credits in their major
Additional Credits Required: 18 credits for Minor Degree

Departments offering Minor Degrees and available combinations:
- Civil Engineering: Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT
- Electrical and Electronics Engineering: Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT
- Electronics and Communication Engineering: Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT
- Electronics Engineering (VLSI): Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT
- Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering: Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT
- Mechanical Engineering: Minor in AI&ML, Cyber Security, Data Science, IoT

Note: Students already enrolled in AI&ML, Cyber Security, or Data Science majors can choose a different minor specialization.
